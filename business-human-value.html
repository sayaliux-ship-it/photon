01.Introduction

ROI is not enough.

Too many AI initiatives are judged only by narrow, short-term ROI measures. But the real opportunity is bigger. AI also enables smarter decisions, lower burnout, and stronger engagement — unlocking value for both the business and the people who make it work.

Consider a call center: AI can resolve routine inquiries, giving agents more time for complex, meaningful conversations. The result is higher customer satisfaction and a better work experience — value that won’t show up in a simple ROI calculation, but is real and measurable.
