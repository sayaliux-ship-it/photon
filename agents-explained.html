Agents

Super agents and sub agents, explained

The Coordinator

A super agent owns an entire business outcome. It decomposes the goal, assigns work to sub agents, mediates between them, handles errors and rollback, and escalates to a human when confidence drops.

The Specialists

Sub agents each do one thing well — ingest a document, verify an identity, score a risk, retrieve knowledge, observe a process.
